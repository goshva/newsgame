Kohli and Gill hundreds take India past 296

Demo article · summary written for the integration demo
Photo

India beat West Indies by eight wickets in the first ODI in Thiruvananthapuram, chasing 296 with 50 balls to spare.

West Indies made 295 for 7 after a 135-run opening stand: Justin Greaves scored 101 and John Campbell 62. Kuldeep Yadav took 4 for 40 and was named player of the match.

Virat Kohli finished unbeaten on 139 off 88 balls, and captain Shubman Gill made 110.

Как игра встроена в эту страницу

     data-article-id="in-ind-wi-odi1"
     data-lang="en"></div>
