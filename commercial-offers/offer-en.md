# NewsGame: short games for your stories

**For:** news publishers and digital media (USA, India, Europe)
**Proposal:** free three-month pilot

## What we offer

We turn your news stories into one- to three-minute games — quizzes, puzzles, arcade — and place them under the article. Readers play without leaving your page. You get a new interactive format without building a games team.

## How it works

1. **One snippet.** You add a container and an async script to your article template. No database, backend or CMS changes.
2. **We build the games.** From tested templates, using facts from your article. Every fact has a quote and a link. Your editor approves, edits or rejects each game.
3. **Readers play, you measure.** The game loads only after a click, in an isolated frame. If anything fails, the block hides and the article is unaffected.

## The free pilot includes

- Integration support and a test site ID
- Up to 20 games on your stories, in your language
- Editorial preview, a source for every fact, one-click removal
- A/B test with a control group and a final report: launches, completions, time on page, reading depth, page speed
- Design in your colours and with your logo

## What we do not promise

We do not promise a fixed increase in time on page. We measure the effect against a control group and report it honestly, together with reading depth, errors and page speed.

## Privacy

No personal data is collected by default, no cookies are required, and the widget works with your consent management platform.
