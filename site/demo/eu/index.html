Irish student wins core prize at EU young scientists contest

Demo article · summary written for the integration demo
Photo

Aoibheann Daly, winner of this year's Stripe Young Scientist & Technology Exhibition, has received the Special Jury Award for Youth at the EU Contest for Young Scientists in Kiel, Germany.

The contest ran from 22 to 27 September, with more than 140 young scientists from 36 European and international guest teams.

Her project uses artificial intelligence to help doctors diagnose and treat brain cancer more quickly. She has also been chosen as a finalist at Regeneron ISEF 2027 in Los Angeles.
